## Accessing the Portal and Logging in

The V-FOR-WaTer portal is available at [portal.vforwater.de](https://portal.vforwater.de). Logging in is required to access the workspace, use the available tools, and view the history of your tool runs.

To log in:

1. Click the **Login** button on the portal homepage.
2. You will be redirected to the Helmholtz AAI login page.
3. Choose how you want to sign in:
    - Your institutional account
    - Google
    - GitHub
    - ORCID

If this is your first time logging in, a **user registration form** will appear after you authenticate. Fill in your details to complete the registration and create your V-FOR-WaTer account. Returning users are taken directly to the portal workspace after logging in.

## Sections of the Portal

The portal consists of 2 larger sections - **Home**, and **Workspace**.

**Home** is where users land up when accessing the portal. The **Home** sections gives users the ability to view, visualise, filter, and add data to their Datastore. Understanding the various ways in which users can interact with the data, along with the types of data available in VFORWaTer can be found in [Accessing Data](accessing-data.md).

**Workspace** allows users to interact with various tools to manipulate their data. A user must be logged in to be able to access the Workspace. Navigating through the workspace can be found in [Workspace Navigation](workspace.md). To see how VFORWaTer integrates tools, the current available tools, and how you can use them, check out the general section on [Toolbox](../toolbox/overview.md)
