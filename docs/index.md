# Welcome to V-FOR-WaTer

The virtual research environment V-FOR-WaTer aims at making digitally available environmental data accessible and scientifically usable in an easy-to-use web portal. Environmental scientists get access to data from different sources, e.g. state offices or university projects, and can share their own data through the portal (planned). Integrated tools help to easily pre-process and scale the data and make them available in a consistent format.  These are both implemented by the developers of the portal according to the requirements of the scientific community and can also be contributed in future directly by the portal’s users. The possibility to store workflows together with the tools and respective data ensures reproducible data analysis. Additionally, interfaces with existing data repositories enable easy publication of the scientists’ data directly from the portal.
