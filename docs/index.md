## What is V-FOR-WaTer?

V-FOR-WaTer brings together environmental data, computational tools,
and research workflows in a common digital environment. The goal is that researchers and other interested users can get started with their scientifc worflows with as minimum overhead as possible.

## A research workflow in four steps

<div class="vfw-card-grid">

<div class="vfw-card">

<h3>1. Explore</h3>

Find datasets and computational tools relevant
to your research question.

</div>

<div class="vfw-card">

<h3>2. Configure</h3>

Select data, tools, parameters, and processing
steps to construct your workflow.

</div>

<div class="vfw-card">

<h3>3. Run</h3>

Execute computational workflows using the selected
data and tools.

</div>

<div class="vfw-card">

<h3>4. Reproduce</h3>

Retain the workflow, tools, and data needed to
